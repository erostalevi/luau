# 🗂 Documents, folders and tabs

## Documents

- **New document:** ⌘N. The first line, `# Title`, is its name.
- Documents open in **tabs**. ⌘W closes one, ⌘⇧T brings it back, ⌘1–9 jump to a tab.
- ⌘\ splits the view, so two documents can sit side by side.
- ⌘P quick-opens any document by name.

## Folders

- Drop a document **onto** another in the explorer (⌘⇧E) to nest it. The parent becomes a folder with an `index.md`.
- Drag documents to reorder them, or drag them to another board.

## A kanban too?
