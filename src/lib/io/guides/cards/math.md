# ∑ Math

Luau renders LaTeX with KaTeX.

## Inline

Wrap it in single dollars: the area of a circle is $A = \pi r^2$, and $e^{i\pi} + 1 = 0$.

## Blocks

Use double dollars on their own lines:

$$
\int_0^\infty e^{-x^2}\,dx = \frac{\sqrt{\pi}}{2}
$$

$$
\begin{aligned}
\text{throughput} &= \frac{\text{cards done}}{\text{week}} \\
\text{lead time} &\approx \frac{\text{cards in progress}}{\text{throughput}}
\end{aligned}
$$

$$
\mathbf{A} = \begin{pmatrix} 1 & 2 \\ 3 & 4 \end{pmatrix}
$$

Insert a block from the slash menu with `/math`. A dollar followed by a digit, like $5, stays plain text.
