# 📣 Callouts

A callout is a quote that starts with `[!kind]`. The text after the kind becomes its title.

> [!note]
> A plain note. `> [!note]`

> [!tip] Tip
> Green, for good advice. Also `[!success]`.

> [!info] Info
> Blue, for context. Also `[!question]`.

> [!warning] Careful
> Amber, for things to watch. Also `[!caution]`.

> [!danger] Danger
> Red, for things that break. Also `[!error]` and `[!bug]`.

Insert one from the slash menu with `/callout`.
