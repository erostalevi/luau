# ✍️ Text and headings

The first line, `# Title`, is always the title of the card. Below it, write normal Markdown.

## Headings

Use `##` to `######` for sections. They also become anchors that other cards can link to.

## Emphasis

- **Bold** with `**bold**` (⌘B)
- _Italic_ with `*italic*` (⌘I)
- ~~Strikethrough~~ with `~~text~~`
- ==Highlight== with `==text==` (⌘⇧H)
- `Inline code` with backticks (⌘⇧C)
- _**Bold and italic**_ with `***both***`
