# 🗂 Lanes and cards

A **kanban board** is a folder. Each **lane** is a sub-folder and each **card** is a `.md` file inside it.

## Cards

- **New card:** ⌘N, or the **+** at the bottom of a lane. Enter keeps adding, Esc stops.
- **Open:** click, or select and press Enter. **Peek:** Space.
- **Move:** drag it, or select it and press ⌥ + arrows.
- **Select many:** ⇧-click, or ⌘-drag a box around them.
- **Archive:** ⌘⌫. Archived cards hide at the end of their lane; ⌘⇧A shows them.
- **Groups:** drop a card _onto_ another card to nest it. Tab / ⇧Tab nest and un-nest with the keyboard.

## Lanes

- **New lane:** ⌘⇧N.
- Right-click a lane title to rename it, pick a color, set a WIP limit, collapse it or archive it.
- Drag a lane by its title to reorder.
- ⌘⇧L switches between **columns** and **rows**.
